<h2>Professional Summary</h2>
<p>Geospatial intelligence scientist with 10 years of experience turning satellite, radar and field data into decisions for environmental markets, mining and law enforcement. Builds operational remote sensing systems end to end: SAR and optical change detection, ML land cover models, Bayesian alert systems, OSINT-fused intelligence products and digital MRV (dMRV) tools for Verra VCS carbon projects. MSc in Geoinformation Science and Earth Observation (cum laude), University of Twente (ITC). Combines rigorous uncertainty quantification with Python, Google Earth Engine and LLM-powered automation. Driven to bring together natural science, geospatial technology and data-driven methods to build robust, scalable and scientifically credible solutions to complex, wicked problems.</p>
